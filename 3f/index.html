🔑 Getting In

Door lock

Keypad entry

The door locks automatically whenever it closes. Always take your smartphone — or a note with your passcode — with you when you go out.

  1. Enter your passcode on the keypad.
  2. Press the red ▽ button at the bottom to unlock.
  3. If the door doesn’t open, press the blue □ button at the top to reset the keypad, then enter the passcode again.
Soundproofing

Two doors — keep the inner one closed

The inner door is soundproof, and the room faces a quiet street. The outer door has no soundproofing at all, so please keep the inner door closed at all times.

🤝 House Rules

Noise

Please be quiet on the outdoor stairs

Some neighbors hold the stereotype that Airbnb guests are noisy. On the outdoor stairs to the 3rd‑floor entrance and the outdoor stairs to the 5th‑floor laundry room, please avoid conversations and playing phone audio without earphones.

Inside the room, you’re welcome to have a party — as long as the soundproof door stays tightly closed. This is a poured‑concrete building, so bass frequencies can carry.

After 10:00 p.m.: please turn the volume down when playing dance music or anything bass‑heavy, out of consideration for guests on other floors. Thank you for your understanding.
Deliveries

Uber Eats, Amazon & mail

  • Couriers cannot come up to your room.
  • For food delivery (Uber Eats, etc.), ask the driver to call you when they arrive, then meet them at the 3rd‑floor entrance.
  • If the host is home, we’ll accept Amazon packages for you and leave them in front of your door.
  • If a missed‑delivery slip is left in the mailbox, we’ll attach it to your door.
Caution

Front‑right window

A previous guest forced this window shut without pressing the button on the support rod, so it has become slightly stiff to open and close.

Always press the button on the support rod when opening or closing the window, and handle it gently — forcing it may jam it completely.

🍳 Kitchen

Glassware

Heat‑resistant glass cups

The glass cups on the shelf are made of heat‑resistant glass. Please use these for hot drinks like tea and coffee.

Kettle

T-fal Electric Kettle (Justine Lock, 1.2 L)

Fill the kettle between the 0.3 L and 1.2 L (MAX) marks, set it on the power plate, and press the power switch down. The pilot lamp lights while it boils, and the kettle switches off automatically when the water is ready.

Hot water lock: when the lock button is raised, no water comes out of the spout. Press the lock button to unlock just before pouring, and keep it locked the rest of the time.

Please note: never fill above the MAX line, and be aware that the hot water lock does not lock the lid — the lid can still open if the kettle is tipped over.
Microwave

Microwave & electric oven

The control panel lights up when you open the door. See the annotated photo for what each button does.

Refrigerator

TOHOTAIYO refrigerator — winter freezing caution

If the room temperature falls below 5 °C, the entire inside of the refrigerator may freeze. In winter, set the temperature control dial (top right inside the fridge) to 1, and keep anything you don’t want frozen on the lower shelves.

Stove

IH cooking heater (built‑in)

Manual: SANKA IH cooking heater (PDF)

Stove

Portable IH cooking heater

  • To turn it on, press and hold the power button for 2 seconds.
  • If you don’t operate it for 3 minutes after turning it on, it switches itself off.
  • Use the − / + buttons to lower or raise the heat.
  • Do not use the plain heat mode as a deep‑fry mode — use the dedicated fried‑food button for frying.
Rice cooker

SHARP rice cooker

  1. Measure rice with the clear measuring cup, put it in the inner pot, and add water: up to line 1 for one cup, line 2 for two cups, line 3 for three cups.
  2. Press only the start (炊飯) button. When you hear a beep, the rice is cooked.
  3. The rice is kept warm until you press the button again — it tastes better if you let it sit on “keep warm” for 10 minutes or more after cooking.

Manual: SHARP KS‑CF05B (PDF, Japanese)

📺 Media & Cleaning

Display

Monitor & Chromecast

The display is already connected to a Chromecast. Turn on the display and select the HDMI input: HDMI 1 = Chromecast, HDMI 3 = HDMI cable (for your own device). When an image appears, that’s the Chromecast screen.

To cast from your smartphone:

  1. Make sure your smartphone and the Chromecast are on the same Wi‑Fi network.
  2. Open the Google Home app on your smartphone (first‑time users: follow the on‑screen setup).
  3. The app lists nearby devices — tap the Chromecast connected to the display.
  4. Once connected, tap the cast icon inside apps like Netflix or YouTube and select the Chromecast.

Detailed guide: How to connect your smartphone to the Chromecast

🧺 Laundry

Service

Laundry service (for longer stays)

Guests staying 7 nights or more may use our complimentary laundry service once per week. Additional loads:

  • 🧺 Half a basket (1 load) — ¥2,000
  • 🧺 Full basket (approx. 2 loads) — ¥4,000
Note: on check‑in days our washer/dryer is fully occupied with linen and towel turnover, so laundry service is not available on the day of arrival. Thank you for your understanding!
How to

Using the laundry basket

  1. Put your laundry in the black circular basket hanging on the wall directly in front of the entrance.
  2. Leave the basket outside the room.
  3. When your laundry is done, it will be returned in the basket in front of your door.

For delicate items that may lose their shape, use the laundry nets in the gray square storage box (stored together with the iron). Fill each net only to about half its capacity so everything dries properly.

Put garbage out at the location shown in the photo. Separate recyclables into four types, each in its own plastic bag — mixed bags will not be collected.

Sorting

How to separate recyclables

  • PET bottles: remove labels and caps, rinse the inside.
  • Plastics: rinse food residue off plastic containers; includes bottle labels and caps, plastic bags, etc.
  • Cans: rinse and bag.
  • Glass bottles: rinse, leave the labels on, put the caps in the can bag.

Official guide: Shibuya Ward Garbage Disposal Manual (PDF)

Special cases

Bulky items & unsorted waste

  • Non‑burnable collection accepts only batteries, broken ceramics, and small appliances.
  • Large items (e.g. suitcases) require an appointment with the Bulky Waste Center: tel 03‑5296‑7000 (buy an oversized‑garbage ticket at a convenience store).
  • Appliances are not accepted — please consult us first.
  • If you can’t separate your recyclables, place them behind the door at the right rear of the building (pictured) and the host will sort them.
